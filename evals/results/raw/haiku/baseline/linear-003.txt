**Step 2 is the first unjustified step.** It moves from "nonnegative" to "positive for every nonzero $x$." Nonnegativity does not give strict positivity. **Step 3 is also unsupported.** Even granting step 2, the argument would give a trivial null space and therefore invertibility, since $Ax = 0$ implies $x^T A x = 0$. The correct statement is: every positive definite matrix is invertible. For PSD matrices, the most one can conclude is that they are invertible if and only if they are positive definite.
